01 / Why 401

The busiest highway in North America runs through your backyard.

The 401 isn't just a road — it's the artery of the GTA economy. A corridor that big deserves a community that's just as serious.

828 km
Stretching from Windsor to the Quebec border — one of the busiest highways on the continent.
416,000+
Vehicles a day through its busiest Toronto stretch, with parts running up to 18 lanes wide.
$600M+
Worth of goods moved through that same stretch every single day.
41,000
Trucks a day keeping that corridor — and the businesses behind it — running.
